# ControlCore

**ControlCore** gives Hytale server owners one in-game panel for everything they configure, and gives other mods
a shared place for their settings, key binds and HUDs.

> **Beta (0.x).** Requires Hytale server **0.7.2 to 0.7.4** (`>=0.7.2 <0.8.0`). Back up `mods/OFATech_ControlCore/` before
> updating.

## What it does

- **Control panel** (`/open-admin-config`): edit the server's `config.json`, every mod's settings, your own
  preferences, key binds and the list of mods, all in game.
- **Player preferences** (`/prefs`): each player chooses their own settings, keys and HUD.
- **Permissions per setting**: let moderators see or change only some settings.
- **Key binds for commands**: bind a key combination to any command, without writing a mod.
- **HUD control**: players hide parts of the game's HUD; admins set rules.
- **Mods tab**: every mod with its logo, version and state; turn mods on or off.
- **Three languages**: English, Portuguese (Brazil) and Spanish, following each player's game language.
